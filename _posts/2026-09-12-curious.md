title: "A call to humankind"
date: 2026-09-12
---

A call to human kind: please remain curious.

Don't be curious because that sounds right. Be **genuinely** curious. Be **authentically** curious. Seek through curiosity for when something comes to mind. Work through curiosity. Through meaningful hard work. Not through its cheaper alternatives.

And learn to listen. Please.

> Give every man thy ear, but few thy voice
> 
> This above all: to thine own self be true"
